Privacy Policy

1918: Ace of Aces · Effective October 19, 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the 1918: Ace of Aces app handles. The short version: the app has no accounts and collects no personal information of its own. The only data involved comes from the two third-party services that make the free version possible — Google AdMob (ads) and Google Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage statistics).

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The app does not require or offer accounts, does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or photos, and does not operate any server that receives your data. Your high scores and settings stay on your device. We do not sell personal data — we never have any to sell.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers and advertising data (such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and ad interaction data) to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob data disclosure. On iOS, ad tracking follows your choice in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt: if you decline, ads are served without cross-app tracking. If you buy the one-time Remove Ads upgrade, ads — and AdMob — are switched off in the app.

Analytics — Google Firebase Analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the game is played and whether features work. It records anonymous usage events only. The events the app sends are:

  • App open — collected automatically by Firebase when the app starts.
  • App shell start — the game's native shell booted successfully.
  • Ad shown — an interstitial or rewarded ad was displayed, and which format it was.
  • Reward earned — a rewarded ad was watched to completion.
  • Remove Ads purchased — the one-time $2.99 upgrade was bought, with the price.

These events carry no names, no account IDs, and no personal identifiers set by us. Firebase may process device and usage information under Google's Firebase privacy documentation and Google's Privacy Policy.

Data retention

Because we run no servers, we retain no data ourselves. Data processed by AdMob and Firebase Analytics is retained by Google according to Google's own policies, linked above.

Children's privacy

1918: Ace of Aces is not directed at children. It depicts realistic war violence — combat, blood, and aircraft being shot down — and it is rated accordingly on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app collects no personal information at all. AdMob's settings for the app are configured for its rating.

Your choices

  • Decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt to limit ad tracking.
  • Use your device's settings to reset or limit your advertising identifier.
  • Buy the one-time Remove Ads upgrade to turn ads off entirely.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
