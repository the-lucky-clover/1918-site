Privacy Policy

1918: Ace of Aces · Effective November 7, 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the 1918: Ace of Aces app handles. The short version: the app has no accounts and collects no personal information of its own. The only data involved comes from Google AdMob (ads) — and only for players who download the app after the launch sale ends, as described below.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The app does not require or offer accounts, does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or photos, and does not operate any server that receives your data. Your high scores, settings, and your launch-day ad-free status stay on your device only — a marker stored locally that is never sent anywhere. We do not sell personal data — we never have any to sell.

Advertising — Google AdMob

From launch through the end of Armistice Day (November 11, 2026), the app is free and shows NO ads. If you download and first play the app during that window, you will never see ads in the app, ever. Two-player co-op never shows ads, for anyone.

After that window, players who download the app may see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers and advertising data (such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and ad interaction data) to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob data disclosure. On iOS, ad tracking follows your choice in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt: if you decline, ads are served without cross-app tracking.

There is no in-app purchase to remove ads. The launch window and two-player co-op are the only ways the app is ad-free.

Data retention

Because we run no servers, we retain no data ourselves. Data processed by AdMob is retained by Google according to Google's own policies, linked above.

Children's privacy

1918: Ace of Aces is not directed at children. It depicts realistic war violence — combat and aircraft being shot down — and it is rated accordingly on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app collects no personal information at all. AdMob's settings for the app are configured for its rating.

Your choices

  • Download and first play the app during the launch window (through the end of Armistice Day, November 11, 2026) and you will never see ads in the app.
  • Decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt to limit ad tracking.
  • Use your device's settings to reset or limit your advertising identifier.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
