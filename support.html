Quick Answers

How do I fly?

Tap and hold to fly and fire. Double-tap the back of your phone to loop-de-loop. Your fuel gauge is your life — it drains as you fly and faster when you're hit. Land at the aerodrome between sorties to refuel.

How does co-op work?

Two-player co-op runs over Wi-Fi between two devices on the same network. One player hosts, the other joins. Co-op never has ads.

What is the day-one promise?

If you download the game from launch through November 12, 2026, your copy is marked day-one and will never show ads. Ever. If you got it free, it's always free.

Does the game collect my data?

The game itself collects no personal data. See the Privacy Policy for the full details, including how advertising works for copies downloaded after the launch window.
