Ace of Aces

The war in the air, rebuilt as an arcade shooter. Fly a SPAD XIII with the 94th Aero Squadron through a 32-sortie campaign over the Western Front — against the German Flying Circus and its brightly painted Jasta squadrons — in a photoreal style no pixel-art shooter ever scratched.

The Campaign

32 Sorties, March–November 1918

A full campaign from the spring offensives through St. Mihiel and the Meuse-Argonne to the final November push — about an hour of flying, sortie by sortie, launch-return-relaunch like the arcade classics.

The Flying Circus

Ten real German aircraft types — Fokker Dr.I triplanes, Fokker D.VIIs in lozenge camo, Albatros, Pfalz, Rumpler, the twin-engine Gotha bomber and more — flown in named Jasta formations with their true squadron colors: Jasta 11 all-red, Jasta 18, 15, 27, 36.

Boss Aces

Every sector ends in a duel. Bosses fly multi-phase patterns and take real punishment to bring down — and the campaign closes with a four-phase finale against the Crimson Ghost.

A Living Ground War

Below you, the war goes on: trenches that fire back, Archie anti-aircraft batteries, A7V tanks, artillery, horse-drawn columns and cavalry. Strafe ground targets for score — every crash leaves a burning debris field the minimap remembers.

Real Horror, Arcade Heart

Every kill dies differently — fireball spins, breaking airframes, smoking glides, pilots bailing out under silk. Snow sorties, burning châteaux, rain-puddled minimaps. One hit kills you, exactly like 1942 taught us.

Built for Two

1P or 2P on one screen, banked sprites for real turning flight, luminous tracer fire by pilot color, and an original chiptune WWI march scored for the title screen — with an engine roar that swells through every loop.

Honest Pricing
