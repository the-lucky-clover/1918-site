1918 WW1

Ace of Aces Dogfight

The war in the air, rebuilt as a photorealistic arcade shooter. Fly a SPAD XIII through 32 sorties over the Western Front — from September 1918 to the Armistice — against German patrols, observation balloons, railway guns, and a fleet of six named Zeppelin raiders.

Coming soon to iPhone

The Campaign

32 Sorties to the Armistice

One continuous campaign: take off, fight, land, tally your score, and take off again — the front scrolls on beneath you from September to 11 November 1918, sortie by sortie, like the arcade classics.

Survival Is Fuel

Your life is a fuel gauge. It drains as you fly, and every hit tears a chunk out of it. Your wingmen fly cover — when a hit would empty your gauge, a wingman takes it instead, and a wingman is gone in a single hit.

Six Zeppelin Bosses

L-33, L-31, L-48, L-59, L-55, and the dreadnought L-70 — each with destructible machine-gun nests, fuel cells, gondolas, and engines. No health bars: read a boss by its scorched fabric, burning engines, and trailing smoke.

A Photoreal Front

Trenches, crater fields, rivers, villages, checkpoints, POW camps, searchlights, and distant artillery duels — every screen, every aircraft, every explosion is photographic. No vector art anywhere.

The Screen Bomb

Grab the blast and it detonates on the spot: every enemy in the sky dies, and even a boss loses 40% of its hull. One pickup. Use it like you mean it.

Built for Two

Two-player co-op over Wi-Fi — two SPADs, one sky, no friendly fire. And an arcade continue system: shot down, you get a 10-second count to get back in the fight.
